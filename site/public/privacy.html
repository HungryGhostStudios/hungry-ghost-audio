Privacy

Effective 27 September 2026.

The store

Cloudflare serves this website and may process network information to deliver it securely. We do not place advertising or tracking cookies on the storefront. Polar handles checkout, purchase records, customer accounts and licence keys under its own privacy policy.

Plugin activation

Activation sends your licence key, activation identifier and a hashed device identifier to our service and Polar. The device identifier is used to manage the two-device activation allowance. We do not collect audio, projects, presets or browsing history. Signed entitlement records are stored locally by the plugin for offline use.

Your records

Use your Polar customer portal to view purchases and manage device activations. Support contact information will be displayed on the storefront when the release opens.
