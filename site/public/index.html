A little help?

Which formats and systems are supported?

The first release targets Windows x64 in VST3 format. REAPER is our primary host for hands-on checks. We’ll list any additional verified hosts in the release notes.

How does licensing work?

A purchase grants two device activations through Polar. Enter your key in the plugin while online. A signed entitlement is cached for offline sessions. You can manage your activations in the customer portal.

What does AGPLv3 source mean?

You have the rights provided by AGPLv3 to study, modify and redistribute the plugin source. Our paid offering covers convenient builds and the store licence service; it does not take away those rights.

Where do I get downloads and support?

Release downloads and support details will appear here when the launch is ready. Purchase receipts and licence keys are available through Polar’s customer portal.
