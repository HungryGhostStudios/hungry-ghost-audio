Once Polar confirms your payment, your receipt and licence key are available in your customer portal. Use the email address you purchased with to sign in.

Get started

  1. Download the installer for your computer from the website. Check each product listing for current Windows and Mac availability.
  2. Close your audio host, install the plugins, then rescan your VST3 folder.
  3. Open the activation button at the bottom of a plugin and enter the HG_ key from your receipt.

Each purchase supports two devices. A suite key unlocks all 51 plugins, including HAUNT in early access. Existing suite owners can activate HAUNT with their current key; no additional purchase is needed. Signed activations support 90 days offline before a refresh.
